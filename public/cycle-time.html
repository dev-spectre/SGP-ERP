Formulas Used

Spindle Speed (N): N = (Vc × 1000) / (π × D)
Material to Remove (E): E = Raw Diameter - Finish Diameter
Diameter Reduction Per Pass (G): G = 2 × Depth of Cut
Number of Passes (M): M = E / G
Time Per Pass (S): S = Length / (Feed × N) [minutes]
Total Cutting Time: T = M × S [minutes]
